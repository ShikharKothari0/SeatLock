# Additional demo events

These are fictional event and venue concepts for SeatLock's portfolio demo.
Prices are suggested INR ticket prices. Select future dates when creating the
events so the catalogue stays useful for demonstrations.

## Blue Hour

- Category: Music
- Venue: The Indigo Room, Mumbai (fictional)
- Duration: 100 minutes
- Suggested start time: 7:30 PM IST
- Seating: Premium, 24 seats at ₹899; Standard, 48 seats at ₹549
- Poster: `/posters/blue-hour.png`

An intimate evening of live jazz, warm lights, and unhurried rhythms. Blue Hour
brings saxophone, piano, upright bass, and drums together for a set that moves
from mellow ballads to lively swing. Settle into your seat and let the quartet
turn an ordinary evening into something memorable.

## The Last Train

- Category: Theatre
- Venue: Lantern Theatre, Pune (fictional)
- Duration: 110 minutes, including an interval
- Suggested start time: 7:00 PM IST
- Seating: Premium, 24 seats at ₹749; Standard, 48 seats at ₹449
- Poster: `/posters/the-last-train.png`

One platform. Five strangers. A departure that never arrives. When an unexpected
announcement leaves a group of travellers stranded at a quiet railway station,
a missing suitcase draws them into a mystery none of them can walk away from.
The Last Train is an atmospheric stage thriller about secrets, chance encounters,
and the stories people carry.

## Orbit

- Category: Family
- Venue: Nova Discovery Auditorium, Bengaluru (fictional)
- Duration: 75 minutes
- Suggested start time: 4:00 PM IST
- Seating: Premium, 24 seats at ₹499; Standard, 48 seats at ₹299
- Poster: `/posters/orbit.png`

Take your seat for a journey beyond Earth. Orbit combines immersive space
visuals, live storytelling, and playful science demonstrations to explore
planets, stars, and the scale of our universe. Designed for curious minds aged
seven and above, this family-friendly stage show makes big cosmic questions
feel a little closer to home.

## Publishing the posters

The PNGs are stored in `frontend/public/posters/`. After they are committed,
pushed, and deployed by Vercel, use these poster URLs:

- https://seat-lock-steel.vercel.app/posters/blue-hour.png
- https://seat-lock-steel.vercel.app/posters/the-last-train.png
- https://seat-lock-steel.vercel.app/posters/orbit.png

These notes and images do not create database records. Create the venues,
layouts, prices, and event drafts through the administrator catalogue, preview
them, then publish.
